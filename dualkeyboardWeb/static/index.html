常见问题FAQ

关于 AI、截图识别、语音文件和会员购买的说明。 Notes about AI, screenshot recognition, voice files, and purchases.

AI 会看到我输入的内容吗？Will the AI service receive what I type?

当你点击生成回复、开始润色或开始变身时，应用会把你主动输入的文本、选择的风格和必要上下文发送到 AI 服务生成结果。请避免输入身份证件、支付信息、账号密码等敏感内容。When you generate a reply, polish text, or transform style, the app sends the text you provide, selected style, and necessary context to the AI service. Avoid entering IDs, payment information, passwords, or other sensitive content.

聊天截图会上传吗？Are chat screenshots uploaded?

截图分析用于从你选择的图片中识别聊天文字。应用会优先使用系统能力在本地提取文字；如你继续使用 AI 生成回复，识别出的文字会随生成请求提交给 AI 服务。Screenshot analysis extracts chat text from an image you choose. The app first uses system capabilities for local recognition. If you continue to AI reply generation, the recognized text is sent as part of that request.

语音分身生成的文件在哪里处理？How are voice persona files created?

语音试听和保存使用系统语音能力生成。你点击保存后，应用会生成 WAV 文件并让你选择保存或分享位置。Voice preview and saving use system speech synthesis. After tapping save, the app creates a WAV file and lets you choose where to save or share it.

应用会展示广告吗？Does the app show ads?

当前项目已接入 Google AdMob，用于开屏、横幅和插页广告。广告相关数据处理以 Google 的规则和本隐私政策说明为准。The current project integrates Google AdMob for app open, banner, and interstitial ads. Ad-related processing follows Google's rules and this privacy policy.
